What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are stored hashed; nobody at GEN Z Foods can read them.
  • Delivery details — recipient name, phone, street address, area, city, nearby landmark and any delivery notes you add at checkout.
  • Order history — what you ordered, when, the amounts charged and the delivery status.

That is the whole list. We do not collect your device location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app requests no permissions beyond internet access.

Why we collect it

To take payment on delivery, cook and deliver your order, let you sign in and see your past orders, and contact you if there is a problem with a delivery. We do not use your data for advertising.

Who we share it with

Our delivery riders see the name, phone number and address needed to complete your order. Beyond that we do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone, and we use no third-party analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account exists. Order records are kept as business and tax records after that, with your personal details removed — see below.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time: in the app, open the Account tab and choose Delete my account. You can also request deletion by email. Full details, and what is removed versus kept, are on the account deletion page.

Security

Traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed. Your login token is held only on your own device and is excluded from Android's automatic backups.

Children

Our services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page.
