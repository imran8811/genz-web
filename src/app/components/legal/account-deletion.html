Your data

Delete your account

You can delete your GEN Z Foods account and personal data at any time. This page applies to the GEN Z Foods Android app (pk.genzfoods.app) and to genzfoods.pk, which share one account.

In the app

  1. Open the GEN Z Foods app and go to the Account tab.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm, then enter your password.

Deletion happens immediately — there is no waiting period.

By email

If you can no longer sign in, email {{ supportEmail }} from the address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify it is you and delete the account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • Your password
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your shopping cart
  • Your sign-in sessions on every device
  • The name, phone number, address and delivery notes recorded on your past orders

What we keep

We keep the order records themselves — the items, amounts, dates and delivery status — with all personal details stripped out, because we are required to retain business and tax records. These records can no longer be linked back to you.

One exception

If you have an order still on its way, we cannot delete the account until it has been delivered or cancelled — the kitchen and your rider still need the delivery details to finish it. Once the order is complete, deletion works normally.

Deletion cannot be undone. To order again afterwards you will need to create a new account.
